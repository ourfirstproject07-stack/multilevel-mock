Extension of Study Room Opening Hours

Dear Student,

We are planning to make some changes to our school’s study room. Currently, the study room closes at 6:00 p.m., but we are considering extending its opening hours until 8:00 p.m. to allow students to use the room in the evening.

We believe this change could benefit students who need extra time to complete their homework or prepare for exams.

Before making a final decision, we would like to hear your opinions about this proposal and any suggestions you may have.

Best regards,
School Administrator

Task 1.1

Write a short email to your friend.

  • Tell your friend about the proposed change to the study room’s opening hours.
  • Explain what you think about this idea.

Write about 50 words.
